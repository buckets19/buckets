      How to play
      • Move: joystick (or WASD / arrows). Up/down moves you deeper or closer on the court.
      • SHOOT: hold, let go in the green. A defender in your face shrinks the green. Run at the rim and press it to dunk.
      • CROSS: quick burst — can break a close defender's ankles.
      • PASS to a teammate (aim with the joystick). In 1v1 it's a pump fake.
      • Defense: BLOCK = jump into the ball while it's going up. STEAL = reach for the ball (get near the dribble). SLIDE = quick step to cut off a drive.
      • STEAL is a reach — the ball handler can CROSS during it to dodge and leave you shook.
      • Hit 3 in a row and you're ON FIRE 🔥 — bigger green until you miss.
      • 16-second shot clock. Ball out of bounds = other team's ball.
      • Keyboard: J/Space shoot·block, K pass·steal, L cross·slide, 1–8 emotes.
